# Frozen B1-LR integration and paired evaluation — 2026-10-07

> **Note, 2026-10-09:** production changed after this record was written. B1-LR is now a
> warning label, not a gate: every Claim that passes the structural checks reaches Judgement
> and the Views, `not_supported` is shown as a plain warning, and the verdict never enters a
> model prompt. The numbers and conclusions below describe the hard gate measured on
> 2026-10-07 and are unchanged. See [B1_INTEGRATION.md](B1_INTEGRATION.md).

The selected research verifier is integrated into the working-tree admission gate. The 20-cluster downstream replay and all 3,296 English/machine-translated Thai benchmark pairs completed. The results show a reduction in unsupported benchmark Claim-ID utilization, with a loss of supported coverage. Thai transfer increases false acceptance, particularly in-domain. These results do not establish native Thai Case accuracy or whole-system factual correctness.

Code baseline: `main` at `812a06763331bb16a5e3a42bfecc6babb61c536b`, with uncommitted integration changes. No training, Thai tuning, new verifier, Reader calls in the experiment, database writes, commit or push. Docker was left stopped; the new image and mounted runtime have not been exercised.

## Actual production boundary

The Case still owns multiple document/narrative Sources. Extraction/OCR provenance, source revisions, exact offsets and page locators remain intact. Formal follow-up answers become addressable Sources for Reader. Reader selects Source-unit IDs; the backend reproduces their original text.

Deterministic binding precedes semantic classification. Eligible Claims pass through the frozen selector/NLI/LR method below. Only `supported` Claims reach Views and Judgement. Parties, Timeline and Impacts are display views derived from admitted Claims, not independent Judgement inputs; their individual fields have no separate semantic classifier in this architecture.

`not_supported` and `unassessed` Claims remain in the trace but are withheld. Zero admitted Claims produces abstention without a Judgement call. Raw follow-up answers cannot bypass the gate. Final summary/gap/MITRE Claim references must identify admitted Claims. Reports reuse the resulting trace and reference binding rather than calling another support verifier.

| State | Exact meaning |
|---|---|
| Source pointer `direct` | Backend resolved a supplied unit ID to original Source text and offsets. |
| Source pointer `recovered` | A legacy/recovery path located an original span; distinct from direct addressing. |
| Source pointer `unresolved` | No accepted Source location. |
| Claim `supported` | Eligible Claim received frozen LR score >= 0.50 against its selected resolved supporting units. Admitted. |
| Claim `not_supported` | Eligible Claim received score < 0.50. Withheld. |
| Claim `unassessed` | No resolved support, unresolved nonduplicate supporting pointer, declared conflicting support, or uncertain epistemic status blocked scoring. Withheld. |
| Reference `bound` | Linked Claim citations resolve. This is addressability, not factual confirmation or independent semantic validation of the referencing statement. |
| `mixed` / `unbound` / `no_claim` | Some linked support resolves / none resolves / no linked Claim, respectively. These are binding states. |

Long inputs now undergo the research-defined longest-first truncation at 512 tokens, with raw length and truncation recorded. Length alone no longer causes `unassessed`. Missing/unverified model assets cause an explicit failure, not admission or a synthetic semantic verdict.

**Residual exposure:** admitted Claims still carry their original supporting citation passages into Judgement, including units not selected for the NLI premise. A proposition attached to a withheld Claim may also appear in another admitted Claim or its citations. Reference checks reject withheld IDs but do not detect all implicit unsupported statements. This experiment therefore measures row-local ID utilization, not complete prevention of unsupported factual propagation.

## Frozen method and identity checks

1. Normalized multilingual MPNet embeddings, cosine >= 0.20; if no unit passes, retain the first maximum as defined by the selected research method. Encoder limit 128 tokens.
2. Preserve original selected text and order; concatenate using one newline.
3. Forward mDeBERTa, `truncation=True`, longest first, maximum 512 tokens; retain the full entailment/neutral/contradiction vector.
4. Apply the frozen WiCE TRAIN-fitted logistic regression, fixed decision boundary 0.50.

## What is measured and what needs semantic gold

Source existence, unit syntax/membership, stale IDs, duplicates, offsets, resolution rates, pointer states, admitted/withheld counts, call counts and elapsed time are deterministic or directly observed. They do not measure semantic support. The real-model pipeline smoke resolves all seven generated unit references and admits three of five Claims; this is a diagnostic fixture, not a population resolution-rate estimate.

Precision, recall, F1, accuracy, false acceptance and false rejection require independent Claim/Source support gold. This study reuses original AttributionBench row-local labels, never verifier-generated labels. Final-prose factual propagation, view-role correctness and native Thai Case accuracy would require additional independent annotation; they are not inferred from ID resolution or classifier F1.

## Downstream replay: design

Twenty label-blind, hash-ordered eligible clusters: ten ID and ten OOD. Eligibility requires at least two distinct Claim texts and nonempty cited references. The slice contains **60 rows: 29 attributable and 31 non-attributable**. Duplicate Claim wording with different row-local references/gold remains separate.

The same original Claims, fixed A IDs, references and gold feed both conditions. No Reader is invoked. No-gate admits every Claim; B1-LR uses frozen archived feature-cache decisions. Claim-link-only admission is equivalent to no-gate in this slice because every selected row has valid links; it is a policy comparison, not an additional live generation arm.

Both arms use the native Judgement prompt and model/configuration: `google/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`, provider list `nextbit/bf16,coreweave/bf16`, thinking budget 8,192, output budget 32,608, temperature 0, English output, no raw QA or RAG input. Provider fallback is disabled. The study explicitly fixes temperature; production otherwise omits an explicit temperature. Prompt SHA-256: `ad4ddb7fc9bf4abce172c7b959910e621ee32f7d9f81f0344b9a824e8bf2bbe2`.

The endpoint is whether an original non-attributable **row's Claim ID** appears in generated summary references. Existing Claim/gold labels are not transferred to synthesized prose. Gaps and MITRE output are not semantically scored. No second final-summary verifier is used.

B1-LR unsupported utilization 95% cluster-bootstrap CI: **4.76–46.67%**. Paired B1-minus-baseline utilization difference: **−77.78 percentage points**, CI **−95.24 to −53.33 points**. Supported utilization CI: **48.28–93.75%**.

At the cluster level, seven paired outcomes lose unsupported-ID utilization and none gains it; exact two-sided McNemar/binomial **p = 0.015625**. The 15-pair sample is small and not a random population sample. ID-only and OOD-only paired tests are inconclusive separately (p=.125 and .25). These results support a reduction in benchmark-ID utilization under this replay, not proof that every unsupported proposition disappears from prose.

There are **40 conditions: 30 completed generations, six failed generations, four gate abstentions**. No-gate: 17 completed, three failed. B1-LR: 13 completed, three failed, four abstentions. Five cluster pairs containing failures are excluded from utilization, with failures reported as unknown rather than safe. Valid abstention outputs contribute zero utilization where their baseline counterpart completes. No successful output cites a withheld/unknown ID.

All six failed stages consumed 40,800 output tokens. Five recorded 40,799 thinking tokens; the other recorded 39,289. Captured failures indicate maximum-token termination. These failures limit downstream observability and do not establish a verifier failure. No Judgement budget or transport change was introduced to improve these results.

## Conclusions justified by these measurements

The production decision implementation matches the selected frozen research classifier. In this 20-cluster slice, semantic admission reduces unsupported benchmark rows entering Judgement from 100% to 29.03%, while retaining 68.97% of supported rows. Across the 15 observable paired outcomes, unsupported row-ID utilization falls from 100% to 22.22%, with supported utilization falling to 70.83%. This is a measured admission/utilization trade-off under the fixed replay.

The frozen English-selected method transfers imperfectly to Google MT-TH: false acceptance rises by 17.39 points ID and 4.39 points OOD. These results warrant caution about Thai deployment claims; they do not justify changing the frozen method after observing test results. The agreed experiments are complete without further model search or tuning.

Unproven: native Thai case-file performance, correctness of independently derived view fields, every claim's truth, legal correctness, semantic correctness of summary/gaps/MITRE/report, prevention of implicit or citation-text leakage, population-wide propagation gains, and runtime compatibility in a newly built Docker image.
